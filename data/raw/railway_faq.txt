Q: How do I check my PNR status?
A: You can check PNR status on the IRCTC website under 'PNR Status', by SMS to 139 with your PNR number, or by calling the helpline 14646.

Q: What is Tatkal booking?
A: Tatkal is a special quota for urgent bookings. AC class opens at 10:00 AM and SL class at 11:00 AM, one day before the journey. Additional Tatkal charges apply.

Q: Can I cancel a Tatkal ticket?
A: Tatkal tickets are generally non-refundable. However, if the train is cancelled or delayed by more than 3 hours, a full refund is provided.

Q: What does WL mean on my ticket?
A: WL stands for Waitlist. Your berth is not confirmed yet. If passengers ahead of you cancel, your WL number drops and you may get confirmed.

Q: What is RAC status?
A: RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) means you have a guaranteed seat but may share a side berth with another passenger. You will get a full berth if a confirmed passenger cancels.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds typically take 5-7 days for credit cards, 3-5 days for net banking, instant for IRCTC wallet, and 1-3 days for UPI.

Q: What is the senior citizen discount?
A: Male passengers 60+ and female passengers 58+ get 50% discount on SL, 3A, and 2A class tickets. 40% on 1A.

Q: How much advance can I book a ticket?
A: You can book up to 120 days in advance excluding the day of departure.

Q: What is dynamic pricing on trains?
A: Premium trains like Rajdhani, Shatabdi use flexi fare where prices increase by 10% for every 10% of seats sold. Maximum is 1.5x base fare.

Q: How many passengers can I book per PNR?
A: Maximum 6 passengers per PNR for normal quota. Maximum 2 passengers per PNR for Tatkal quota.

Q: What is the cancellation fee for 3A class?
A: For cancellations more than 48 hours before departure, the cancellation fee for 3A is ₹180. Between 12-48 hours, it is 25% of fare.

Q: Can I get a refund for WL ticket?
A: If your WL ticket is not confirmed at the time of charting, you automatically get a full refund. No action needed.

Q: What documents do I need for travel?
A: You need a valid government ID: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport.

Q: How do I file a TDR?
A: Go to IRCTC website, My Bookings section, and file a Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) within 72 hours of the journey for partial journey refunds.

Q: What is the helpline number for IRCTC?
A: IRCTC 24x7 helpline: 14646. Railway enquiry: 139.

Q: What is GNWL?
A: GNWL (General Quota Waitlist) is the most common waitlist type, covering all passengers booking from the train's origin station.

Q: What is RLWL?
A: RLWL (Remote Location Waitlist) is for passengers booking from intermediate stations not covered by the General Quota.

Q: My train was cancelled. Will I get a refund?
A: Yes, if your train is cancelled by Railways, you get a full refund automatically without any cancellation charges.

Q: What is the Garib Rath Express?
A: Garib Rath Express is an all-AC sleeper train offering affordable AC 3-Tier travel for budget-conscious passengers.

Q: Can I book tickets without internet?
A: Yes, you can book at any PRS (Passenger Reservation System) counter at railway stations. IRCTC agents also offer booking services.

Q: मेरा PNR स्टेटस कैसे चेक करें?
A: आप IRCTC वेबसाइट पर 'PNR Status' में, SMS से 139 पर PNR नंबर भेजकर, या 14646 हेल्पलाइन पर कॉल करके PNR स्टेटस चेक कर सकते हैं।

Q: Tatkal ticket book karna kab shuru hota hai?
A: AC classes ke liye Tatkal booking subah 10 baje se shuru hoti hai. SL aur 2S classes ke liye 11 baje se. Yeh journey se ek din pehle hota hai.

Q: WL ticket cancel karne par refund milega?
A: Agar aapka WL ticket charting tak confirm nahi hota, to pura refund automatic mil jata hai. Agar ticket confirm tha aur aapne cancel kiya, to normal cancellation charges lagte hain.

A: The penalty for travelling without a valid ticket is ₹250 plus the applicable fare for the journey undertaken.

Q: Can I transfer my confirmed train ticket to a family member?
A: Yes, Indian Railways and IRCTC permit ticket transfer to immediate family members (father, mother, brother, sister, spouse, son, or daughter). You must submit a written application along with your original photo ID proof and proof of relationship (Aadhaar, Ration Card, or Passport) to the Chief Reservation Supervisor at the station at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled departure of the train. For marriage party members or NCC cadets, application can be made 48 hours and 24 hours in advance respectively.

Q: What is the process for transferring a railway ticket to someone else?
A: Railway tickets cannot be transferred to unrelated individuals. However, ticket transfer to blood relatives and spouse (father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, spouse) is permitted by submitting a written request with relationship proof to the Chief Reservation Supervisor at least 24 hours before train departure. Government servants on duty can also request transfer 24 hours prior.

Q: What is the free luggage allowance on Indian Railways?
A: The free luggage allowance per passenger is: AC First Class (1A): 70 kg (marginal allowance 15 kg), AC 2-Tier (2A): 50 kg (marginal allowance 10 kg), AC 3-Tier and AC Chair Car (3A/CC): 40 kg (marginal allowance 10 kg), Sleeper Class (SL): 40 kg (marginal allowance 10 kg), Second Class: 35 kg (marginal allowance 10 kg). Excess luggage exceeding the allowance must be booked in advance in the parcel van at standard parcel rates.

Q: Can I carry excess baggage on a train?
A: Yes, excess baggage beyond the permissible free allowance can be booked in advance in the brake van or parcel office at standard parcel tariff rates. Carrying unbooked excess luggage in the compartment can attract a penalty of up to 6 times the normal luggage rate.

Q: What are the child fare rules on Indian Railways?
A: Children under 5 years of age travel free without a separate berth. For children between 5 and 11 years: if a separate berth/seat is requested, full adult fare is payable; if no separate berth is requested, half of the basic adult fare plus full reservation charge applies. Children aged 12 years and above are charged full adult fare with full berth entitlement.

Q: Do children need tickets on trains?
A: Children under 5 years of age travel free and do not require a separate reservation or berth. Children between 5 and 11 years require a ticket: choose between half fare (without berth) or full adult fare (with dedicated berth).

Q: What should I do in case of a medical emergency during train travel?
A: In a medical emergency, immediately notify the Train Superintendent, Conductor, or TTE, or dial Railway Helpline 139 / tweet to @RailMinIndia. All passenger-carrying trains have first-aid boxes with emergency life-saving medicines and doctors in the train are requested for assistance. A railway medical officer will be summoned at the next scheduled halt for urgent treatment or hospital evacuation.

Q: Are doctors available on Indian Railway trains?
A: While dedicated doctors are not stationed in every coach, TTEs and train staff carry emergency first-aid kits and can announce across coaches for traveling medical professionals. For critical medical situations, train controllers arrange for a railway doctor and ambulance to meet the train at the next major station halt.
